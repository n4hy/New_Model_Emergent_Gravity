\section{Finite Balls}

finite, universal, state-independent bilinear form linear in the axial
source. By the absorbability proposition of Paper IV this form is
absorbed into a redefinition of the local modular term---equivalently,

improvement the finite-ball matching is identical in structure to the
null-plane matching. Consequently the same value of $c_2$ continues to
satisfy the matching for every ball.

Because the family of equations (one per ball) is overdetermined, and

therefore fixed for the entire program.
