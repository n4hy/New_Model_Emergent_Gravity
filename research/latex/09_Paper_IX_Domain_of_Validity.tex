\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Papers I--VIII of the Emergent Gravity Program establish, inside
holographic conformal field theories with AdS asymptotics, that the
finite-ball entanglement first law is equivalent to the linearized
Einstein--Cartan equations (metric sector unconditional, axial sector
after a finite dictionary improvement) and that the Hehl--Datta
coefficient is fixed by pure information. The present note records the
final open question of the program: the domain of validity of these
results beyond AdS. Every \pP{}-grade derivation relies on the
Casini--Huerta--Myers modular Hamiltonian for balls, a global conformal
structure, and a clean asymptotic boundary. None of these ingredients
is known to exist in the same form in de Sitter space or in a generic
cosmology. The AdS results stand independently; their extrapolation to
cosmology remains \pO{}. Labels follow the standing convention of the
program.

Why this work is required. A claim that ``the gravitational sector of
known physics is entanglement bookkeeping'' is incomplete until its
domain is stated with the same precision as its derivations. The AdS
sector is now closed. The cosmological sector is not. This note isolates
the precise obstructions and the minimal requirements for a future
derivation, so that the public record of the program is accurate and no
stronger claim is made than the evidence supports.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Epistemic Conventions}
Every material claim is tagged \pP{} (proved/derived within a stated
framework), \pC{} (conjectured with support) or \pO{} (open).
Numerical agreement is not proof. Consensus is not proof.

\section{What Has Been Established}
Inside the holographic AdS regime the following statements are certified:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em]
\item The first law of entanglement on all balls is equivalent to the
linearized Einstein equations about AdS (Faulkner--Guica--Hartman--Myers--Van
Raamsdonk). \pP{}
\item The modular perturbation kernel is exact and machine-verified. \pP{}
\item The metric sector of the torsionful first law for finite balls is
unconditional by the conformal selection rule. \pP{}
\item The axial channel produces a universal kinematic residue $I_B$ that
is non-vanishing for finite regions and absorbable into a finite
dictionary improvement. \pP{}
\item The sign of the Hehl--Datta coefficient follows from relative-entropy
positivity. \pP{}
\item The magnitude is fixed both by rigidity under program normalizations
and by pure-information Fisher--canonical matching (null-plane limit +
absorbability). \pP{}
\end{enumerate}
Collectively these results establish the Einstein--Cartan certification
of the New Standard Model from entanglement bookkeeping inside the AdS
domain.

\section{The Final Open Question}
Almost every step above uses one or more of the following ingredients
that are special to AdS/CFT:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item the Casini--Huerta--Myers modular Hamiltonian for ball-shaped
regions, which relies on a conformal map to a hyperbolic geometry;
\item a global conformal structure on the boundary;
\item a clean asymptotic boundary with a well-defined holographic dictionary;
\item the existence of a Ryu--Takayanagi surface and a bulk entanglement
wedge with a Killing horizon dual to the modular flow.
\end{itemize}
None of these is known to possess a direct counterpart of equal rigor
in de Sitter space. In de Sitter:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item there is no conformal boundary of the same type;
\item static-patch modular Hamiltonians exist but are not given by a
CHM-type local integral of the stress tensor in the same way;
\item the absence of a global timelike Killing vector outside the static
patch complicates the notion of a first law for arbitrary ball-like
regions;
\item no generally accepted microscopic dual exists that would supply an
analogue of the FGHMV equivalence.
\end{itemize}
Cosmology in this program is a \emph{metric} question. Torsion is
algebraic, vacuum-vanishing, and does not propagate: it is not a
cosmological limitation and is not part of the domain bar. The open
claim is that the same bookkeeping produces Einstein gravity with
$\Lambda$ in a cosmological setting. That claim is at present \pO{}
at the FGHMV standard (sign and isometries; Paper~17). The spinless
vacuum of the metric sector, when $\Lambda>0$, \emph{is} de~Sitter;
that is Einstein$+\Lambda$, not a holographic selection of cosmology.

\section{Minimal Requirements for Closure}
A derivation that would close the \emph{metric} domain question at the
standard of the AdS results must:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em]
\item identify a family of regions in de Sitter (or a quasi-local
cosmological horizon) whose modular Hamiltonians are under analytic
control;
\item establish a first-law identity relating the variation of
entanglement entropy to a bulk Noether charge or canonical energy,
with the sign derived, not copied from AdS;
\item show that the resulting constraints are equivalent to the
linearized Einstein equation with $\Lambda$,
$G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=\kappa T_{\mu\nu}$.
\end{enumerate}
Torsion and Hehl--Datta are not items on this list. Until such a
derivation exists, the holographic extrapolation remains open. The
vacuum solution itself does not wait on that derivation.

\section{Certified Status of the Program}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item AdS / holographic regime with ball regions: closed. \pP{}
\item de Sitter / generic cosmological regime: open. \pO{}
\item Non-linear / non-perturbative completion: open. \pO{}
\item UV completion of the effective Lagrangian: open by construction
(the program is a theory of everything we know, not a theory of
everything). \pO{}
\end{itemize}
The AdS results stand on their own. No claim of the program that has
been labeled \pP{} is weakened by the domain limitation. The limitation
is simply recorded with the same precision that the derivations
themselves were recorded.
