\section{What Is Closed}
Inside the holographic AdS regime with ball-shaped regions:
the finite-ball torsionful first law holds (metric sector unconditional,
axial sector after finite dictionary improvement); the Hehl--Datta
coefficient is fixed in sign by relative entropy and in magnitude both
by rigidity and by pure information; the kinematic residue $I_B$ is
non-vanishing and of relative size $O(1)$, and is absorbable.

\section{What Remains Open}
Multi-digit numerical value of the coefficient of $I_B$ (analytic
Hadamard subtraction still required). Extension beyond AdS (de Sitter /
cosmology). Non-linear Einstein--Cartan equations from entanglement.
UV completion of the effective Lagrangian.

These limitations are recorded with the same precision as the positive
claims. The program is a theory of everything we know inside its stated
domain; it is not a theory of everything.
