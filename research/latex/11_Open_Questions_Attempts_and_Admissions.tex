\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The Emergent Gravity Program's Final Status table of 14 August 2026
closes the linearized Einstein--Cartan certification inside holographic
AdS and leaves four items open: the multi-digit coefficient of the
kinematic residue $\IB$, the de~Sitter / cosmological domain, nonlinear
Einstein--Cartan from entanglement, and UV completion of the effective
Lagrangian. This note attempts each item in turn.

One computation is performed: a $d=2$ hard-cutoff subtraction of the
coincident $1/(x-y)^2$ piece of the flowed chiral-current two-point
function against the modular kernel
$\pi/(2\cosh^2(\pi s))$. Three grids give a relative residue
$r\in\{-0.656,-0.622,-0.645\}$, spread $0.034$. That is consistent
with the order-unity claim already on the books. It is \emph{not} a
multi-digit coefficient. Paper~VIII already stated that further
numerical proxies of this class will not produce one. This note
confirms that statement by failing in the same way.

The other three items are not computed. A literature audit shows:
(i)~no Casini--Huerta--Myers modular Hamiltonian of equal rigor exists
for generic de~Sitter regions; (ii)~the Faulkner--Haehl--Hijano--Parrikar--Rabideau--Van~Raamsdonk
second-order derivation \cite{FHHPRV2017} yields Einstein's equations,
not Einstein--Cartan, and Jacobson's entanglement-equilibrium proposal
\cite{Jacobson2016} remains a conjecture for the full nonlinear theory;
(iii)~UV completion is open by construction of the effective Lagrangian.
Each failure is recorded as an \pA{}, not papered over.

This note does not move any Final Status label from \pO{} to \pP{}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Attempt I: the multi-digit coefficient of $\IB$}
\label{sec:ib}

\subsection{Definition}

Paper~VI writes the surviving axial channel as
$N_B[\beta;\zeta]=C_J\,\IB[\beta;\zeta]$ with
\begin{equation}
\IB[\beta;\zeta]
=
-\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} ds\,
K(s)\,
\bigl\langle
\sigma_s J_5[\beta]\,
\widetilde{J}_5[\zeta]
\bigr\rangle_{\kappa\neq 0}.
\label{eq:IB}
\end{equation}
In $d=2$ chiral CFT the interval modular flow is the M\"obius action
\cite{McGwierVI}
\begin{equation}
x(s)=R\tanh\bigl(\mathrm{artanh}(x/R)+\pi s\bigr),
\label{eq:flow}
\end{equation}
the current two-point function is
\begin{equation}
\langle J(x)J(y)\rangle = \frac{k}{(x-y)^2}
\label{eq:JJ}
\end{equation}
for a normalization $k$ that cancels in any relative residue, and the
weight-one Jacobian is $J(s,x)=\partial x(s)/\partial x$.

The local quadratic form is the $s=0$ value of the smeared correlator.
The relative residue reported below is
\begin{equation}
r
:=
\frac{\displaystyle\int ds\,K(s)\bigl(Q(s)-Q(0)\bigr)}{|Q(0)|},
\qquad
Q(s)
=
\int\!dx\,dy\,\beta(x)\beta(y)\,
\frac{J(s,x)}{\bigl(x(s)-y\bigr)^2}.
\label{eq:r}
\end{equation}
A compactly supported bump
$\beta(x)=\exp\bigl(-1/(1-(x/a)^2)\bigr)$ for $|x|<a<R$ is used.

\subsection{What a Hadamard extraction would be}

Write the flowed two-point function as a distribution on
$x,y\in(-R,R)$. A Hadamard expansion isolates
\begin{equation}
\langle\sigma_s J(x)\,J(y)\rangle
=
\frac{u_2(s;x,y)}{(x-y)^2}
+
\frac{u_1(s;x,y)}{x-y}
+
v(s;x,y)\log|x-y|
+
w_{\mathrm{fin}}(s;x,y),
\label{eq:hadamard}
\end{equation}
with $u_2,u_1,v,w_{\mathrm{fin}}$ smooth, then subtracts
$u_2,u_1,v$ by a finite-part prescription that commutes with the
$s$-integral against $K(s)$. The number of record would be the
integral of $K(s)w_{\mathrm{fin}}$ (smeared), independent of the
cutoff to the quoted digits.

\subsection{What was actually computed}

A hard spatial cutoff $\varepsilon$ on $|x(s)-y|$ was imposed and
$r$ was evaluated on three grids
(\texttt{m9\_2\_ib\_hadamard.py}):

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
\toprule
$(N_x,N_s,s_{\max},\varepsilon)$ & $r$ \\
\midrule
$(201,61,2.0,0.04)$ & $-0.6555$ \\
$(241,81,2.5,0.03)$ & $-0.6216$ \\
$(281,81,2.5,0.02)$ & $-0.6450$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Spread: $0.034$. Kernel integral on these grids is $1$ to
$O(10^{-3})$ (truncation in $s$). The three values lie in the
order-unity window already reported by Paper~VIII (``approximately
$1$--$5$ depending on the finite-part implementation''). The sign
here is an artefact of reporting $r$ as
$\int K(Q-Q_0)/|Q_0|$ with this Jacobian convention; the structural
statement is $|r|=O(1)$, not a signed high-precision coefficient.

\subsection{Why this is not a Hadamard extraction}

\begin{admission}
The $u_1$ and $\log$ terms of \eqref{eq:hadamard} were not isolated.
The $s$-dependence of $u_2$ was not subtracted. The cutoff
$\varepsilon$ remains visible at the third digit. No three-digit
agreement exists across the three grids. Paper~VIII already closed
the numerical campaign for this reason. Repeating a discarded proxy
does not reopen it. The multi-digit coefficient of $\IB$ remains
\pO{}.
\end{admission}

A mutation that replaced $K(s)$ by a flat measure on $[-s_{\max},s_{\max}]$
would change $r$ by an $O(1)$ factor; that check was not needed to
establish failure, because the scheme spread already exceeds any
honest claim of ``digits.''

\subsection{What would close Q1}

An analytic computation of $w_{\mathrm{fin}}$ for the M\"obius flow
of $\langle J(x)J(y)\rangle$, or a numerical scheme whose finite part
is stable to $\ge 3$ digits under simultaneous refinement of
$(N_x,N_s,\varepsilon)$ and under at least two inequivalent
subtractions (Hadamard vs.\ differential regularization). Until one
of those exists, no label change is licensed.

\section{Attempt II: the de~Sitter domain}
\label{sec:ds}

\subsection{What AdS supplies and de~Sitter does not}

Paper~IX lists four ingredients used by every \pP{} step of the
series \cite{McGwierIX}:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em]
\item the Casini--Huerta--Myers modular Hamiltonian for balls, which
is a conformal image of the Rindler boost \cite{CHM2011};
\item a global conformal structure on the boundary;
\item a clean asymptotic boundary with a holographic dictionary
\cite{Maldacena1998};
\item a Ryu--Takayanagi surface whose modular flow is a bulk Killing
horizon \cite{RT2006,HRT2007}.
\end{enumerate}

de~Sitter has a cosmological horizon and a Gibbons--Hawking entropy
\cite{GH1977}. It does not have (1)--(4) in the same form. Static-patch
modular Hamiltonians exist as operator statements about the static
observer algebra; they are not given by a CHM-type local integral of
$T_{00}$ on an arbitrary ball. There is no generally accepted
microscopic dual that would supply an FGHMV equivalence
\cite{Strominger2001}. The classical static-patch first law carries a
characteristic minus sign: adding Killing energy \emph{decreases}
horizon entropy \cite{Banihashemi2022}. That sign is already a
structural obstruction to copying the AdS bookkeeping line by line.

\subsection{What was attempted}

No de~Sitter modular Hamiltonian was derived in this note. No
static-patch first-law $\Leftrightarrow$ linearized Einstein$+\Lambda$
map was written. Torsion was not the missing object.

A literature pass confirms that recent work on de~Sitter entanglement
(timelike extremal surfaces, $c$-theorems in a fixed de~Sitter
background, static-patch algebra) does not supply the four items of
Paper~IX. Citing those papers as if they closed Q2 would be a
category error.

\begin{admission}
Q2 is unattempted as a derivation and unclosed as a theorem. The
AdS results stand. Their extrapolation to cosmology remains \pO{}.
This note adds no new de~Sitter identity.
\end{admission}

\subsection{What would close Q2}

The metric checklist of Paper~IX \cite{McGwierIX}: controlled modular
Hamiltonians on a family of de~Sitter regions; a first-law identity
with a derived sign; equivalence to linearized Einstein$+\Lambda$.
Torsion is not on the list. Anything weaker than that bar is a hope
as a holographic theorem. The spinless vacuum of Einstein$+\Lambda$
with $\Lambda>0$ is already de~Sitter and does not wait on the bar.

\section{Attempt III: nonlinear Einstein--Cartan}
\label{sec:nl}

\subsection{What exists for Einstein gravity}

At first order, FGHMV \cite{FGHMV2014} is the theorem the program
uses. At second order, Faulkner, Haehl, Hijano, Parrikar, Rabideau
and Van~Raamsdonk \cite{FHHPRV2017} show that if ball entanglement
entropies of a restricted class of CFT states are represented by the
HRRT formula in an asymptotically AdS geometry, that geometry
satisfies Einstein's equations through second order, with matter
determined by the CFT one-point functions, under the central-charge
relation $\widetilde{C}_T=a_\ast$. The derivation uses the Hollands--Wald
identity \cite{HollandsWald2013} and the quantum Fisher /
canonical-energy matching of Lashkari--Van~Raamsdonk
\cite{LV2016}.

That is a real theorem about \emph{Einstein} gravity. It is not a
theorem about Einstein--Cartan gravity. The HRRT functional is an
area. The Hollands--Wald charge used in \cite{FHHPRV2017} is the
Einstein--Hilbert Noether charge. Torsion does not appear.

Jacobson's entanglement-equilibrium argument \cite{Jacobson2016}
proposes that the full nonlinear Einstein equation is the
equilibrium condition of entanglement on small balls, for a
maximally symmetric vacuum. The series already tags the transfer of
that argument to general backgrounds and to torsion as \pC{}.
Thermodynamic derivations with torsion exist at the Clausius level
\cite{Dey2017}. They are not FGHMV-grade equivalences.

\subsection{What was attempted}

No second-order Einstein--Cartan identity was derived. No
Hollands--Wald charge for the Palatini--Holst or
Einstein--Cartan Lagrangian was written. No ball-by-ball matching
of Fisher information to a torsional canonical energy at $O(\lambda^2)$
was performed.

\begin{admission}
Q3 remains \pO{}. The honest strongest statement this note can make
is: nonlinear Einstein gravity from entanglement is a published
theorem through second order \cite{FHHPRV2017}; nonlinear
Einstein--Cartan from entanglement is not. Promoting the former to
the latter by ``the same bookkeeping'' is a conjecture, and the
series already refused to call it a theorem.
\end{admission}

\subsection{What would close Q3}

A derivation, at the standard of \cite{FHHPRV2017} or of FGHMV, that
the finite-ball entanglement first law (or relative entropy through
second order) is equivalent to the nonlinear Einstein--Cartan
equations, including the algebraic Cartan equation for torsion, with
the Hehl--Datta contact generated rather than inserted. Until that
exists, the nonlinear regime stays \pO{}.

\section{Attempt IV: UV completion}
\label{sec:uv}

The New Standard Model is asserted to be emergent bookkeeping for a
deeper description \cite{McGwierIII}. The deeper description, in the
certified regime, is a holographic pair. No first-principles
selection of that pair exists. Gauge group, representations,
Yukawas, generations, and $\Lambda$ are empirical input.

\begin{admission}
Q4 is open by construction. This note does not propose a CFT, a
string compactification, an asymptotic-safety fixed point, or any
other UV completion. Inventing one to fill the section would be a
fabrication. The label remains \pO{}.
\end{admission}

A useful, and weaker, statement that \emph{is} available: the
effective Lagrangian \eqref{eq:nsm} below is an EFT whose cutoff is
at most $M_{\mathrm{Pl}}$, whose new interaction is Planck-suppressed,
and whose gravitational sector is certified only at linear order in
AdS. That is a domain statement, not a completion.

\begin{equation}
S_{\mathrm{NSM}}
=
\frac{1}{4\kappa}
\int\varepsilon_{abcd}\,e^a\wedge e^b\wedge R^{cd}
-
\frac{\Lambda}{6}
\int\varepsilon_{abcd}\,e^a\wedge e^b\wedge e^c\wedge e^d
+
\int e\,\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{SM}}.
\label{eq:nsm}
\end{equation}

\section{What remains worth doing}

The only open that is a computation rather than a missing framework
is Q1. The next honest attempt is analytic: expand
$\langle J(x(s))J(y)\rangle$ for the flow \eqref{eq:flow} in the
Hadamard form \eqref{eq:hadamard} and integrate $K(s)w_{\mathrm{fin}}$
against a Schwartz source. If that integral can be reduced to a
standard special function, the coefficient exists as a number. If
the finite part depends on a renormalization scheme that the
modular kernel does not fix, the coefficient is not universal and
that fact should be recorded as a result, not hidden.

Q2--Q4 require ideas that this note does not contain.

Torsion, in Einstein--Cartan, is algebraic, locked to spin density,
and zero in vacuum. That much is a theorem of the Cartan equation.
It does not follow that spacetime torsion is a spintronic field:
the Hehl--Datta contact is still $\sim G$ and collider-invisible.
Effective geometric connections in materials are a different object.
Unpolarized FLRW spin averages down, so standard late cosmology
does not see this torsion; that is an estimate, not a no-go for
every extension. The de~Sitter open is a metric-domain problem.
Do not confuse the three.
