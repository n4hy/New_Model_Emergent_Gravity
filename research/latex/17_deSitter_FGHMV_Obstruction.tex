\begin{abstract}
\noindent

minus sign in the static-patch first law and did not attempt a
derivation. This note \emph{decides} Q2 at the only standard the
program has used for a \pP{} gravitational theorem: Faulkner--Guica--
Hartman--Myers--Van Raamsdonk.

That standard cannot be met in de~Sitter by copying the AdS
argument. Two identities are enough.

The Gibbons--Hawking entropy of four-dimensional de~Sitter is
$S=\pi\ell^2/G=3\pi/(G\Lambda)$. Then $\partial S/\partial\Lambda<0$:
more vacuum energy, smaller horizon, less entropy
\cite{GibbonsHawking1977}. The cosmological-horizon first law has a
minus sign: adding Killing energy \emph{decreases} horizon entropy
\cite{Banihashemi2023}. The Casini--Huerta--Myers integrand is
$+T_{00}$. Copying the AdS bookkeeping line to the cosmological
horizon uses the wrong sign. \pP{}

Independently, $\dim\mathfrak{so}(1,4)=10$ while the conformal
algebra that supplies CHM special conformal maps in four dimensions
is $\mathfrak{so}(2,4)$, of dimension $15$. de~Sitter isometries do
not contain the CHM family of arbitrary balls. \pP{}

No dual is constructed. Jacobson is not used as a substitute.
Linearized Einstein$+\Lambda$ from a cosmological CFT remains
unproved and is not labeled \pP{}. What \emph{is} \pP{} is that
FGHMV-standard closure of Q2 is obstructed.

The AdS results stand.

\section*{Standard of proof}

``Close de~Sitter'' at this program's grade means the four-point
checklist of Paper~IX: a family of regions with controlled modular
Hamiltonians; a first-law identity; equivalence to linearized
Einstein--Cartan; Hehl--Datta from relative-entropy positivity.

Jacobson's entanglement equilibrium \cite{Jacobson2016} is not that
grade (Paper~14). Strominger's dS/CFT \cite{Strominger2001} is
Euclidean and generically non-unitary, and is not a Maldacena pair.
This note does not promote either.

A negative decision at the stated standard is a decision.

\section{The AdS first-law sign}

Casini--Huerta--Myers for a ball of radius $R$ in a CFT vacuum is
\cite{CHM2011}
\begin{equation}
H_B
=
2\pi\int_{\lvert x\rvert<R}\!d^{d-1}x\,
\frac{R^2-\lvert x\rvert^2}{2R}\,T_{00}(x).
\label{eq:chm}
\end{equation}
The kernel of $T_{00}$ is positive inside the ball. Faulkner et
al.\ convert $\delta S_B=\delta\langle H_B\rangle$ into linearized
Einstein equations about AdS with the standard energy sign
\cite{FGHMV2014}: a positive $\delta\langle T_{00}\rangle$
increases ball entanglement entropy. Relative-entropy positivity
locks that direction.

\section{The de~Sitter identities}

Four-dimensional de~Sitter of radius $\ell$ has
$\Lambda=3/\ell^2$ and a cosmological horizon of area
$A=4\pi\ell^2$. The Gibbons--Hawking entropy is
\cite{GibbonsHawking1977}
\begin{equation}
S_{\mathrm{GH}}
=
\frac{A}{4G}
=
\frac{\pi\ell^2}{G}
=
\frac{3\pi}{G\Lambda}.
\label{eq:gh}
\end{equation}
Differentiating at fixed $G$,
\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial S_{\mathrm{GH}}}{\partial\Lambda}
=
-\frac{3\pi}{G\Lambda^2}
<0.
\label{eq:dsign}
\end{equation}
Increasing the vacuum energy decreases the horizon entropy.
Banihashemi, Jacobson, Svesko and Visser isolate the same minus
sign in the static-patch first law: adding Killing energy reduces
cosmological-horizon entropy \cite{Banihashemi2023}.

\begin{theorem}[sign obstruction]
\label{thm:sign}
The FGHMV first law and the Gibbons--Hawking static-patch first law
have opposite signs for the response of entropy to Killing energy /
vacuum energy. A line-by-line copy of the AdS bookkeeping onto the
cosmological horizon is therefore false. \pP{}
\end{theorem}

A mutation confirms the derivative is not tautological. For AdS,
$\Lambda=-3/\ell^2$ and the same area law $S=\pi\ell^2/G$ give
$\partial S/\partial\Lambda>0$ along that branch. The de~Sitter
sign is the one that conflicts with \eqref{eq:chm}.

\section{The isometry obstruction}

The isometry algebra of $\mathrm{dS}_4$ is $\mathfrak{so}(1,4)$,
of dimension $10$. The conformal algebra of four-dimensional
Minkowski space, which supplies the special conformal
transformations used to map an arbitrary ball to a Rindler wedge
in the CHM construction, is $\mathfrak{so}(2,4)$, of dimension
$15$.

\begin{theorem}[too few isometries]
\label{thm:iso}
$\dim\mathfrak{so}(1,4)=10<15=\dim\mathfrak{so}(2,4)$. The
de~Sitter isometry group does not contain the CHM family of
arbitrary-radius balls. The only regions with an exact Killing
modular flow for a de~Sitter-invariant state are static patches
(and their complements), not a FGHMV net of balls. \pP{}
(Lie-algebra dimension; the static-patch clause is the standard
Killing-horizon statement, cited as such).
\end{theorem}

de~Sitter is conformally flat, so a \emph{CFT living on a fixed}
$\mathrm{dS}_4$ background can still use $\mathfrak{so}(2,4)$.
That is entanglement of matter \emph{on} de~Sitter, not a
derivation of the Einstein equation with $\Lambda$ as dynamics.
Q2 is the latter. Conformal flatness does not supply a holographic
pair, a timelike boundary dictionary, or a Ryu--Takayanagi theorem
at the FGHMV grade.

\section{What the small-ball limit does prove}

For a ball of proper radius $R\ll\ell$ in a fixed de~Sitter
geometry, $g=\eta+O(R^2/\ell^2)$ and $H_B$ is the Minkowski CHM
plus $O(R^2/\ell^2)$. The first law in that limit is the Minkowski
/ AdS one. It does \emph{not} derive
$G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=\kappa T_{\mu\nu}$ as an identity
from a family of finite balls. The cosmological constant is
invisible until $R\sim\ell$, which is exactly the regime in which
Theorem~\ref{thm:sign} applies.

The monograph's estimate $\Lambda L^2\ll 1$ below the Hubble scale
\cite{McGwierMonograph} is unchanged and is not a dS derivation.
