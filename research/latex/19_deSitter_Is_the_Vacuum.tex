\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Cosmology in this program is metric. Torsion is algebraic and
vanishes wherever the spin density vanishes, so it is not a
cosmological limitation.

The metric sector of the New Standard Model, in vacuum, is
Einstein's equation with cosmological constant. If $\Lambda>0$
and $T_{\mu\nu}=0$, the unique complete simply-connected
maximally symmetric solution is de~Sitter space of radius
$\ell=\sqrt{3/\Lambda}$. That identity is \pP{}. It is Einstein
gravity, not an entanglement selection of our universe.

Faulkner--Guica--Hartman--Myers--Van Raamsdonk cannot be copied
onto the cosmological horizon (Paper~17). Jacobson does not
become \pP{} for the Standard Model (Paper~18). Those two facts
are why ``emergent gravity yields de~Sitter'' is not a prize
theorem. The vacuum is de~Sitter. The bookkeeping does not yet
select it.
\end{abstract}

\section{The vacuum}

When the spin density vanishes, the Cartan equation forces
torsion to zero and the NSM metric equation is
\begin{equation}
G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=\kappa T_{\mu\nu},
\qquad
\kappa=8\pi G.
\label{eq:EE}
\end{equation}
Set $T_{\mu\nu}=0$ and $\Lambda>0$. The unique (up to isometry)
complete simply-connected Lorentzian 4-manifold of constant
positive curvature is de~Sitter space, with
\begin{equation}
\Lambda=\frac{3}{\ell^2},
\qquad
R_{\mu\nu}=\Lambda g_{\mu\nu},
\qquad
G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=0.
\label{eq:dS}
\end{equation}
In the flat FLRW chart this is $a(t)=e^{Ht}$ with $H^2=\Lambda/3$,
which saturates both Friedmann equations at vanishing density.
\pP{} (Einstein$+\Lambda$; machine-checked).

Torsion does not appear. A late-universe spin average is an
estimate about matter, not a correction to \eqref{eq:dS}.

\section{What this is not}

It is not a derivation of $\Lambda$ from entanglement. It is not
an FGHMV theorem about a cosmological CFT. It is not Hehl--Datta.
It is not a reason to award a Nobel Prize.

A prize-grade claim would be: the entanglement first law, with a
\emph{derived} sign, is equivalent to linearized
Einstein$+\Lambda$ about de~Sitter in the universe we inhabit.
Paper~17 proves that copying the AdS argument is false. That gap
is the one that still decides the case. This note does not fill
it.
