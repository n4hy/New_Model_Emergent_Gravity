\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The same A1 logic as Paper~21, now an area law on the diamond waist
of Papers~22--23. Peschel entropy
$S=-\mathrm{Tr}[C_A\log C_A+(1-C_A)\log(1-C_A)]$,
lattice area $A_{\mathrm{cut}}=$ bonds leaving the ball,
$S=\alpha A_{\mathrm{cut}}+\beta$.

$N=16$, $R=2,3,4,5$: $\alpha(0)=0.245$. UV masses with
$mR_{\max}\le 0.5$ shift $\alpha$ by at most $4.3\%$.
Area-law RMSE $0.27$ versus volume-law $2.96$.
Auditor, $N=14$, $R=2,3,4$: $\alpha(0)=0.245$, relative drift
$2.1\%$. C2 PASS. \pP{} as a necessary-condition test on this field.

The unsubtracted sea density is $\varepsilon=-0.970$ per site.
That is not mean-zero curvature. It is not quantum foam. It is
not $\eta=1/4G$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Gate}

Locked before the run. $C1$: $\alpha(0)>0$. $C2$: relative UV
drift $<20\%$ for $mR_{\max}\le 0.5$. $C3$: area fit beats volume
fit at $m=0$. $C4$: at least three radii.

An independent adversary on $N=13$ (odd, $37$ exact zero modes)
found $\alpha(0)=0.245$ and a worst UV drift of $5.9\%$. C2
stands. Catch: C3 is observable-dependent. Cut bonds beat volume.
Boundary-site count and $4\pi R^2$ need not. Absolute $\alpha$
depends on the area proxy. The locked proxy was cut bonds.

\section{Foam and mean curvature}

The destination I want is a quantum foam whose \emph{mean}
curvature vanishes. That is not a result of this note.

If the mean is an infrared statement, it is $\Lambda=0$ and the
vacuum is Minkowski. That is not de~Sitter (Paper~19 needs
$\Lambda>0$). If the mean is only of ultraviolet fluctuations
about a smooth background, it does not select $\Lambda$.

The free lattice sea has $\varepsilon\simeq -1$ in lattice units.
Identifying that $\langle T_{00}\rangle$ with Einstein's source is
the cosmological-constant problem, not a foam theorem. Setting
$\langle{:T:}\rangle=0$ by normal ordering inserts a zero mean
by definition. I will not call that a derivation.

\section{What this does not do}

It does not produce $\eta=1/4G$. It does not make Jacobson 2016 a
four-dimensional theorem. It does not copy FGHMV onto de~Sitter.
It does not finish the foam. A2 (Paper~23) plus this A1 are
necessary-condition tests on a free $3{+}1$D fermion. They are
not victory.
