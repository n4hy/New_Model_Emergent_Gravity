adversary). Mixing bulk and surface lets a flat kernel ride
the area-law surface. Scoring the cut alone is the Clausius
probe. The bulk run was locked first and lost. The surface
run uses a different $N$ so the $N=12,R=4$ surface numbers
are not promoted.
