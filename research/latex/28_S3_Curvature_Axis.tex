\begin{abstract}
\noindent
A guess: virtual modes live on a $3$-sphere whose fourth
embedding axis is imaginary and whose values are curvature, so
the same sphere has de~Sitter and anti-de~Sitter regions. A
guess is not a habitat and not a result. The geometry underneath
it is real.

Round $S^3$ of radius $\rho$ has Ricci scalar $6/\rho^2$,
computed from the metric. $\rho\to i\ell$ sends
$\mathcal{R}\to-6/\ell^2$. One family, two signs. The equator
is a round $S^2$. The Haar mean of the embedding coordinate
$X_4=\cos\chi$ is identically zero. All four identities \pP{}.

Identifying $X_4$ with curvature, and putting virtual particles
on this $S^3$, is a guess. It does not copy FGHMV onto
de~Sitter: $\dim\mathfrak{so}(1,4)=10<15=\dim\mathfrak{so}(2,4)$
is still Paper~17. It does not measure $\Lambda$.
\end{abstract}

\section{The $3$-geometry}

\[
\mathrm{d}s^2=\rho^2(\mathrm{d}\chi^2+\sin^2\chi\,\mathrm{d}\Omega_2^2).
\]
Christoffel reduction of this metric gives $\mathcal{R}=6/\rho^2$.
The substitution $\rho=i\ell$ is algebraic and flips the sign.
That is the spherical / hyperbolic pair: $S^3$ and $H^3$.
Calling the two signs dS and AdS \emph{areas} is allowed as a
name for the two 3-geometries. They are not yet $4$d de~Sitter
and anti-de~Sitter spacetimes.

The equator $\chi=\pi/2$ is $S^2$. Papers~23--27 already work
on a 2-sphere topology (the diamond waist). That is a kinship
of topology, not a derivation of those lattice results from
this embedding.

\section{Mean zero}

On the unit $S^3$, $\langle\cos\chi\rangle=0$ by oddness of the
Haar integrand. If $X_4$ \emph{is} curvature, the uniform mean
is zero. That is the foam picture I want, \emph{given} the
identification. It is not a derivation of the identification,
and it does not select $\Lambda>0$. A mean-zero axis is
Minkowski as the average, with both signs as fluctuations.

\section{What this does not move}

Paper~17 stands. Paper~19 still says the spinless
Einstein$+\Lambda>0$ vacuum is de~Sitter, as a metric identity.
Paper~27 still says the CHM envelope beats a flat kernel on the
\emph{cut}, with weak tracking. This note records a guess and the identities it sits on.
It does not finish the selection.
