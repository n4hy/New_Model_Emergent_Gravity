\begin{abstract}
\noindent
A guess: virtual modes live on a Bloch $3$-sphere, so the
modular hop should track a Bloch vector, not the CHM weight.
The testable piece is scored on \emph{every} nearest-neighbour
dimer in the ball.

For the $2\times 2$ correlator of a dimer,
$r_x=2C_{ij}$, $r_z=C_{ii}-C_{jj}$.
$N=16$, $R=5$: $1302$ dimers. $N=12$, $R=4$: $624$ dimers.
Auditor $N=14$, $R=5$: $1302$ dimers.

$R(K,r_x)\approx 1.00$, $\lvert\rho\rvert\approx 0.03$.
$R(K,w_{\mathrm{CHM}})\approx 0.16$, $\rho\approx -0.987$.
$r_x$ is spatially flat. $\langle r_z\rangle=0$.

C2 FAIL. Auditor REFUTED. The Bloch rewrite does not describe
$K$. CHM does. This covering is dense enough to end that
argument. Curvature as a Bloch axis was not claimed and is
still a guess.
\end{abstract}
