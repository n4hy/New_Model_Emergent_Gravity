\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Hop-variation first laws stalled (Papers~26--31). Jacobson varies
the diamond, not the Hamiltonian. This note holds $H$ and the
vacuum correlator fixed and changes only the region.

Balls $R=2,3,4,5$ fit $S=\alpha A_{\mathrm{cut}}+\beta$ with
$\alpha=0.245$ (Paper~24). RMS residual $0.27$. Cubes about that
same line: RMS $5.67$. Taxicab diamonds: $9.46$. A one-site shift
of the $R=4$ ball changes $S$ by $0.06\%$.

Ball $R=2$ and taxicab $t=2$ have the \emph{same} cut area $78$
and $S=13.60$ versus $12.00$. Area law cannot see that.

C2 PASS. Auditor $N=14$: $0.29$ versus $3.93$, CONFIRMED.
The finite piece of $S$ knows shape. The ball is the tight
family. That is the CHM region's distinction, as a lattice
fact, not as Einstein.
\end{abstract}
