only eleven $R=2$ balls contained both masses. This

residual $0.053$. CHM: $0.955$ and $0.298$. The boost
weight varies ten times more than enclosed energy, and
$\delta S$ follows the small variation.

Auditor, $47$ both-inside balls: C2b REFUTED.

Paper~37's CHM win mixed ``in the ball'' with ``where
in the ball.'' Well inside, the first law is Gauss.
Not $8\pi G$. Not Einstein. Not de~Sitter.
