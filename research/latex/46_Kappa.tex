\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Paper~45 found that a large ball's $\delta S$ tracks
enclosed energy. This note measures the ratio.

One mass, $120$ well-inside balls: med~$\kappa=0.968$,
rel~IQR $0.084$. Two masses, $69$ balls: $0.984$ and
$0.099$. Relative shift $1.7\%$. The one-mass $\kappa$
predicts the two-mass $\delta S$ at Pearson $0.999$.

Auditor, $N=10$: $0.972$ vs $0.992$ ($2.1\%$), CONFIRMED.

A reusable Gauss first-law constant, to about two percent.
Not $1/4G$. Not $G_{\mu\nu}=8\pi GT_{\mu\nu}$. Not de~Sitter.
\end{abstract}
