\begin{abstract}
\noindent
A compact packet is a star: $\delta S$ grows by $13\%$
from $R=2$ to $R=5$ and then sits. A wide packet
($\sigma=8$) grows by a factor $16$. Both track
enclosed energy (Pearson $0.999$).

Nested volume and area are collinear. That is not a
volume-versus-area theorem.

The wide $\delta e$ is not a uniform fluid
($\mathrm{std}/\mathrm{mean}=0.35$). Inherited Poisson
does not give $a\propto r$. Auditor CONFIRMED the first
law and REFUTED linear $a$.

The first law can tell a star from an extended source
without Poisson. It did not give de~Sitter.
\end{abstract}
