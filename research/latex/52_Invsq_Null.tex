\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Paper~50 cancelled at the midpoint. The masses were
equal. That is a symmetry, not a barycenter theorem.

Unequal packets, $M_B/M_A=2.46$. Between two attractors
the force balances at $M_A/r_A^2=M_B/r_B^2$, closer to
the lighter mass. The measured zero sits $0.0025L$ from
that point and $0.16L$ from the centre of mass.

Auditor, $N=10$: the side (toward the light mass, not
the CM) CONFIRMED. The $0.04L$ precision REFUTED ---
fatter packets, near-field zero.

I first asked for the centre of mass. That was the
wrong Newton. This note keeps the correction.
\end{abstract}
