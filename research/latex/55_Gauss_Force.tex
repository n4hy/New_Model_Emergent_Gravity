\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Papers~48--54 solved a Dirichlet box to get
$\mathbf{a}$. This note does not. Spherical Gauss
and the first-law mass $M=\delta S/\kappa$ give

\[
a(R)=-M(R)/R^2.
\]

A compact packet: $M$ plateaus, slope of $|a|$ vs
$R$ is $-1.997$ on $R=3,4,5$. A periodic band-edge
sea: $M$ grows with volume, slope $+1.266$.

Auditor, $N=10$: $-1.889$ and $+1.237$, CONFIRMED.

I kept Gauss. I dropped the box. I did not derive
Einstein, and I did not write a de~Sitter dual.
\end{abstract}

\begin{admission}
I stopped solving Poisson. The first-law mass and
Gauss already knew the two laws. I did not call
that a derivation of $G_{\mu\nu}=8\pi G T_{\mu\nu}$.
\end{admission}
