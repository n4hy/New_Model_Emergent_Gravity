\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Metric SdS has $T\mathrm{d}S+\mathrm{d}M=0$. This note
asks the lattice complement of an enclosing ball.

The Fermi sea is pure: $S_0(B)=S_0(B^c)$. The packet
puts $\delta S(B)=+0.196$ and
$S_{\mathrm{global}}=0.196$. The new entropy is in
the ball. $\delta S(B^c)$ is $10^{-5}$ or smaller,
plus at $R=3$, minus at $R=4,5$.

That is not a cosmological first law. Auditor
$N=10$: the same $10^{-5}$ minus. The magnitude
is the result, not the sign of the dust.

I did not call a $10^{-5}$ remainder $T\mathrm{d}S+\mathrm{d}M=0$.
\end{abstract}
