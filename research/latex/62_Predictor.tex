\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Paper~37 gave CHM the modular kernel. Paper~45 gave
enclosed energy the well-inside first law. This note
scores both, plus $\mathrm{Tr}(K_{\mathrm{vac}}\Delta C)$,
as predictors of finite $\delta S$ on $216$ balls.

$\alpha=0.005,0.02,0.08$. $P_{\mathrm{flat}}$ wins
every time ($0.991$, $0.996$, $0.999$). CHM and
$T_K$ fall as $\alpha$ grows. Well-inside: the same
winner. Auditor $N=10$: $P_{\mathrm{flat}}$ at both
$\alpha$. No flip.

Finite $\delta S$ tracks $\sum\delta e$. That is the
same object Paper~61 uses as mass. The CHM win is
about $K$, not about this $\delta S$.
\end{abstract}

\begin{admission}
I put the three predictors on the same balls. Energy
won. I did not flip the winner to save Paper~37.
\end{admission}
