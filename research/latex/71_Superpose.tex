\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The pair field of Papers 69--70 is
$\mathbf g=-(M/A)\hat n$ with
$\hat n=\nabla_c M/\lvert\nabla_c M\rvert$.
That operation is not linear:
\[
\mathbf g_{AB}\propto M_{AB}\,
\frac{\nabla M_A+\nabla M_B}{\lvert\nabla M_A+\nabla M_B\rvert}
\neq
\mathbf g_A+\mathbf g_B
\]
unless $\nabla M_A\parallel\nabla M_B$.

Exact open-hop basis, \texttt{mpmath} $dps=80$,
no eigensolve. Mass maps add to
$2\times 10^{-7}$. Median angle
$\angle(\mathbf g_{AB},\mathbf g_A+\mathbf g_B)=5.43^\circ$.
Relative vector residual $0.255$: C\_rel fails.
Far from both packets the angle is $0.008^\circ$.
Auditor $N=11$: map and angle confirmed; residual
$0.45$ refuted.

To use two packets: $M=M_A+M_B$, then one
$\hat n=\nabla_c M$. Not Einstein.
\end{abstract}
