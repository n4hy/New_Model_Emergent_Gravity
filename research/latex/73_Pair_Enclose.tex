\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Paper 72 tracked pair $\delta S$ on $R=3$ balls that
only touch the source sites. This note uses $R=5$.
Exact open-hop basis. Peschel is \texttt{mpmath eigsy}
at $80$ decimals. No hop LAPACK.
\[
S_{\mathrm{global}}=2h(\alpha_A)+2h(\alpha_B).
\]

Midpoint enclosing ball: $f_S=0.9907$, $f_E=0.9881$.
A one-site axial offset still contains both sites
but leaks the heavier packet: $f_S=0.886=f_E$ to
half a percent, not $1$. A ball on $A$ with $B$
outside: $f_S=0.382$, $f_E=0.386$.
Auditor $N=11$: midpoint $0.994$; a perpendicular
offset stays at $0.990$; miss $0.306$. Primary
enclosure confirmed. Axial $f=1$ fails as leak.

Pair $\delta S$ is pair mixing entropy times the
enclosed mass fraction. Not Einstein.
\end{abstract}
